# People Analyzer

Quarterly (leadership) or annual (full company) cultural-fit + role-fit scoring.

## Naming Convention

`YYYY-MM-DD.md` per round. The date is the round's start date.

## The Four Quadrants

| | Right Seat | Wrong Seat |
|---|---|---|
| **Right Person** | Invest, develop, promote. The bedrock. | Move them. Find the right seat. |
| **Wrong Person** | Talented but values-misaligned. Hardest case. Usually has to go. | Compounded problem. Has to go. |

## The Bar

Minimum acceptable threshold. Start at 3 pluses, 2 plus-minuses, 0 minuses out of 5 Core Values. Raise over time.

## Cadence

- **Quarterly:** leadership team (light re-check every 90 days, full at the quarterly off-site).
- **Annually:** every person in the company (typically alongside the Annual Planning Day).
- **Off-cycle:** before any promotion or seat change. When a Core Values violation becomes visible.

## Discipline

The Visionary and Integrator go first. A leadership team that does not score themselves lacks moral authority to score anyone else.
