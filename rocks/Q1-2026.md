# Quarterly Rocks: Q1 2026

*1-3 SMART 90-day priorities per person. Set quarterly, reviewed weekly.*

---

> **How to use this file.** This is the TEMPLATE shape for a quarterly Rocks file. Once your business is actually in Q1 2026 (or Q2 2026 or whichever current quarter), the `eos-set-quarterly-rocks` skill writes a real file like `rocks/Q2-2026.md` with your actual Rocks. Naming: `Q{YYYY}-{Q}.md`. Reviewed every week in the L10 [Rock Review](https://traction.wiki/concepts/rock-review) segment. 80% completion is the target.
