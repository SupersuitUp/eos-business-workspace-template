# Issues List

*Master inventory of unresolved obstacles, ideas, and opportunities.*

---

> **How to use this file.** Every unresolved obstacle, idea, opportunity, or open decision goes here. The weekly L10 works through this list via IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve). Capture issues as they emerge, even mid-meeting (the "Drop It Down" cue). Updated via `eos-run-level-10` and `eos-ids-single-issue`.
